``PhotospherePlotter.image()`` now places the stellar surface so that its circumferential equatorial radius equals the ``radius`` parameter, as in the rest of X-PSI. Previously, ``radius`` was used as the value of the radial coordinate at the equator, which gave a circumferential radius about 0.2% larger at 600 Hz, so imaging results change slightly (by up to about 0.4% in flux in tests).

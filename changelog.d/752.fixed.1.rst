The ``TLMIN`` and ``TLMAX`` keywords are now read from the header entries of the right column (``CHANNEL`` in PHA files, ``F_CHAN`` in RMF files) instead of fixed keyword names such as ``TLMIN1`` or ``TLMIN4``. When they are missing, ``Data.from_pha`` uses the first channel of the file and ``Instrument.from_ogip_fits`` uses the first channel of the ``EBOUNDS`` extension, with a message. Previously, channels were wrongly numbered with ``checkqual`` and counts wrongly grouped with ``checkgrp`` when the channels did not start at 0.

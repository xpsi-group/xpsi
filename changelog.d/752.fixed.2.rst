Fixed the grouping of event data in ``Data.from_evt``, which crashed with both ``minbincnt`` and ``checkdatasrc``. Bad-quality channels are now removed before the remaining channels are grouped, as in ``Data.from_pha``, and a last group with exactly ``minbincnt`` counts is now kept. With ``checkdatasrc``, the grouping of the source data is used and ``minbincnt`` defaults to the source value.

The cluster panels of ``ResidualPlot`` (``plot_clusters=True``) were reorganised: the residuals above ``threshlim`` and the cluster sizes are now shown in a single panel sharing the phase axis of the panels above (first and second phase cycle respectively), with their colour bars above the panel. The cluster sizes use the same channel axis as the residuals, a new sequential colormap ``'PuOr_seq'`` (new default of ``clusters_cmap``), discrete colours when all clusters are smaller than 7 pixels, and pixels outside clusters are left blank. The cluster-size histogram was replaced by the cumulative number of clusters with size at least *s*, compared with the noise-only range.

``ResidualPlot`` (with ``plot_clusters=True``) and ``Residual1DPlot`` now show a reference normal distribution, by default N(0,1) (set with the new ``mu`` and ``sigma`` arguments of ``Residual1DPlot``), together with the p-value of a Kolmogorov-Smirnov test of the residuals against it. The test results are stored in ``residual_ref_ks_stat`` and ``residual_ref_ks_pvalue``; ``ResidualPlot`` also stores the fitted normal parameters in ``residual_fit_mu`` and ``residual_fit_sigma``.

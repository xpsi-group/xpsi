Fixed ``CustomInstrument.from_ogip_fits`` in modules written by the module generator with ``--use-fits-format``. It now calls ``xpsi.Instrument.from_ogip_fits`` instead of keeping its own copy of the loader. Before this fix, RMF files whose channels start at 1 (``TLMIN = 1``, e.g. Chandra) either failed to load or silently lost the last channel and got channel energy edges shifted by one, and RSP files with a ``SPECRESP MATRIX`` extension could not be read.

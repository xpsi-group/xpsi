Fixed several problems in the image-plane ray tracer used by ``PhotospherePlotter.image()``: imaging could hang for rapidly rotating stars with a non-zero quadrupole (as given by the default ``'universal'`` relation); the total flux could be wrong (by a factor of four in one test) because an area weight was read from outside its array; sky maps were offset by one pixel; and the emission angle omitted an off-diagonal term of the surface metric.

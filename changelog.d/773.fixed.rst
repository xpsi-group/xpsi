Fixed several problems in the image-plane ray tracer used by ``PhotospherePlotter.image()``: imaging could hang for rapidly rotating stars with a non-zero quadrupole (as given by the default ``'universal'`` relation); the total flux could be wrong by an unpredictable amount (between negligible and a factor of four too low in tests) because the area of the outermost ring of the image-plane grid was computed from memory outside its array; sky maps were offset by one pixel; and the emission angle omitted an off-diagonal term of the surface metric.
